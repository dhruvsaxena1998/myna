Summary

Three-person review of the Meetings module. The group agreed that mic capture must follow the meeting app's mute state and that unknown states are treated as muted. Rename-once propagation and remembered voices were confirmed. Screenshot deduplication by perceptual hash was accepted as the default.

Decisions

  • Zoom mute mirroring moves to phase 2.

# 08: Screenshots and window video

**Status:** draft
**Spec stories:** 25–28, 37
**Depends on:** 05

Screenshots of the meeting window every 15 s with duplicates dropped, or window video, shown as a filmstrip.

## Stories
- As a user, I want the meeting window auto-picked from the app holding the mic, with an override.
- As a user, I want screenshots every 15 s, near-duplicates dropped, shown as a filmstrip with click-to-seek.
- As a user, I want to choose off, screenshots or window video, with a size estimate for video.

## Done when
- [ ] Screen Recording permission flow handled
- [ ] Filmstrip with click-to-seek
- [ ] Video plays in sync with audio
- [ ] Checked on a real call
